My Guiding Journey

I first joined Girl Guides in 2022 as a member of Cedar Girls’ First Company.

Guiding has given me many opportunities and has been a place where I learnt a lot. I learnt how to be respectful, how to step up when needed, and how to let go at times to allow my juniors to lead and try instead of doing everything for them. Guiding has shaped my leadership and facilitation skills, helping me become the person and leader I am today.

Currently continuing my Guiding journey as a Young Adult with Girl Guides Singapore Headquarters.

My Guiding Experiences

NDP 2023 @ Padang

Through the hours spent at rehearsals, I made many new friends and sharpened my foot drills. My sense of belonging to Guides and Singapore grew stronger than ever before, and I now put on my uniform with greater pride.

Facilitation Workshop 2023

Through this workshop, I learnt how to facilitate sessions for my juniors and level mates.

Patrol Leader Training Course 2023

PLTC was a turning point in my Guiding journey. I learnt to work with and lead people from different backgrounds, understand their needs, and become a better leader both within and outside of Guides.

Promotion 2024

The Promotion Ceremony brought mixed feelings. I felt the excitement and sense of responsibility that came with holding one of the highest ranks, Assistant Company Leader. At the same time, I feared not meeting expectations and disappointing my unit and Guiders.

Camp Fest 2024 @ Camp Christine

I learnt to work with and lead people I was not familiar with, such as Brownies from other schools. I learnt to listen to and understand them, especially since it was the first overnight camp for many of them and they were feeling homesick.

The heavy rain and thunderstorm also woke us up on the first night, and we had to quickly pack our belongings from the tents and bring them under shelter. I also learnt from my camp commandants to put others before myself.

NDP 2024 — In-School Contingent Commander

Together with the flag bearer from my unit, I had to teach and improve our contingent’s foot-drill standard within a short period of time. We experimented with different teaching methods to achieve a respectable standard of foot drills during the ceremony in front of our schoolmates.

Baden-Powell Award — July 2024

The Baden-Powell Award (BPA) is the highest award presented by our Chief Commissioner to a Girl Guide. To attain it, a Guide needs to obtain at least 70% in both the written and practical components.

GIRL Adventure: Maldives 2024–2025

Girl Guides sent a contingent to the Maldives for a five-day, four-night camp in November 2024 to learn more about the climate issues faced by the country, many of which are similar to those faced by Singapore.

We aim to complete impact projects, such as collaborating with the National Library Board and organising intertidal-zone walks to educate the public and help combat climate change.
